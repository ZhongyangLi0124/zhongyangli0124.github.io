Hi! I am a first-year CS PhD student at [**Ohio State University**](https://www.osu.edu/), advised by Prof. [Huan Sun](https://u.osu.edu/ihudas/people/) and working with [**OSU NLP**](https://x.com/osunlp?lang=en). Previously, I was a Master's student at [**Johns Hopkins University**](https://www.jhu.edu/), advised by Prof. [Ashutosh Dutta](https://ashutoshdutta.org/), and a research intern at the [**University of Maryland, College Park**](https://www.umd.edu/), working with Prof. [Tianyi Zhou](https://tianyizhou.github.io/). My research interests center on efficient foundation models, with a specific focus on Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) and Test-Time Adaptation. My work aims to improve models' generalization and efficiency. My current research focuses on **LLM agents**.
