Hi there, I am a first-year CS PhD student at [**Ohio State University**](https://www.osu.edu/), where I am advised by Prof. [Huan Sun](https://u.osu.edu/ihudas/people/). Previously, I was a Master's student in Computer Science at **Johns Hopkins University**, advised by Prof. [Ashutosh Dutta](https://ashutoshdutta.org/), and a research intern at the **University of Maryland, College Park**, working with Prof. [Tianyi Zhou](https://tianyizhou.github.io/). My research interests center on efficient foundation models, with a specific focus on Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) and Test-Time Adaptation. My work aims to improve models' generalization and efficiency. My current research focuses on **LLM agents**.
